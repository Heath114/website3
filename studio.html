Naqsh.

An independent design studio in Jabal Al Weibdeh, Amman. Eleven people working across brand, web and product.

Since 2016

How we got here

Naqsh started in 2016 with two people at a shared desk in Weibdeh. Rana Khoury had spent eight years as an art director at advertising agencies in Amman and Dubai, and Lina Barakat joined from a digital agency six months later.

We moved into our current studio on Fawzi Al-Malouf Street in 2020. The team is now eleven: six designers, a strategist, three developers and a studio manager.

Our clients range from start-ups to hospitality groups and clinics, and around a third of our work is for companies in the Gulf.
