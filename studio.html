An independent design studio in Amman. Eleven people who care a little too much about kerning, and a lot about whether the work actually moves the needle.

Since 2016

How we got here

Naqsh started in 2016 as two people and a shared studio desk. Rami had spent years art-directing for agencies that treated design as decoration; the idea was simple - build a studio that treated it as strategy.

We grew slowly and deliberately. Today we're eleven - designers, a strategist, developers - and we still take on only a handful of projects at a time so each one gets the attention it needs.

We work with founders, marketing teams and the occasional stubborn dreamer. What they have in common is a refusal to look like everyone else.
