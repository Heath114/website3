Fakhar Ceramics

فخار

A two-person pottery studio in Madaba selling at weekend markets. We gave them an identity with the character of their work and a shop to sell it year-round.

The brief

Nour and her brother make tableware by hand in a studio off King Talal Street. Their work sold out at every market, and then there was nothing to buy until the next one.

What we did

The symbol is a thrown vessel, and the wordmark is set in Bricolage Grotesque, which is slightly irregular in the way handmade pottery is. The identity is applied with rubber stamps rather than printing, so every box is a little different. The online shop tells you who made each piece and when it came out of the kiln.
